PharmaRoute

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PharmaRoute ("we," "our," or "us") provides a delivery management platform for pharmacies, including a driver mobile app and a dispatcher web dashboard (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

By using PharmaRoute, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information from users of the Service (drivers, pharmacy managers, and pharmacy owners):

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Role (e.g. driver, manager, org owner)

Location Information

  • Precise GPS location, collected while using the driver app, to build delivery routes, calculate estimated arrival times, and provide live tracking to dispatchers

Delivery Information

  • Delivery (patient) addresses associated with pharmacy orders
  • Photos captured as proof of delivery
  • Delivery status, timestamps, and notes entered by drivers or managers

Earnings Information

  • Driver pay rate, pay type (hourly, per-delivery, or per-km), and calculated earnings based on completed shifts and deliveries

Identifiers

  • A unique account identifier is assigned to each user to manage authentication and associate data with the correct account

We do not collect payment card information, health or medical records, contacts, browsing history, or any data unrelated to pharmacy delivery operations.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate core app functionality, including account authentication, route building, and delivery tracking
  • Allow pharmacy dispatchers to assign, monitor, and manage deliveries and drivers
  • Calculate and display driver earnings
  • Notify patients about the status of their delivery, where an email address has been provided for that purpose
  • Maintain records of completed deliveries, including photo proof, for accountability and dispute resolution
  • Improve the reliability and safety of the Service (for example, using weather data to warn drivers of hazardous conditions along a route)

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your information to third parties.

3. How Information Is Shared

Information is shared only as necessary to operate the Service:

  • Within your organization — a pharmacy's dispatchers and managers can see information related to drivers and deliveries within that pharmacy's organization
  • Service providers — we use third-party infrastructure providers (such as Google Firebase for data storage and authentication, Google Maps for routing and geocoding, and OpenWeather for weather data) to operate the Service. These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of PharmaRoute, our users, or others

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention

We retain account, delivery, and earnings information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed to maintain accurate business and delivery records. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us using the information below.

5. Your Choices

  • You may update your account information (such as your name) at any time through the app or by contacting your pharmacy's dispatcher
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email below
  • Location access can be disabled through your device settings, though this will limit or disable core app functionality such as route building and live tracking

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission and access controls that restrict data to authenticated users within the appropriate organization. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

PharmaRoute is intended for use by adult drivers and pharmacy staff. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above when changes are made. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
