Optimized Route Planning

How it works

From prescription to doorstep

01
Scan the label
Driver scans the prescription label with the PharmaRoute app. AI reads the address automatically.
02
Get your route
Tap Start Driving. PharmaRoute builds the optimal route and opens Google Maps with all stops loaded.
03
Deliver & confirm
At each stop, take a photo or get a signature. The patient gets notified automatically.
04
Dispatcher sees all
Pharmacy staff track every driver and delivery in real time from the web dashboard.
